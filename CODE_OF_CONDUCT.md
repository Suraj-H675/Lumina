# Code of Conduct

Lumina contributors must maintain a respectful, inclusive, evidence-based environment.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique work, not people.
- Welcome beginners.
- Explain technical and scientific reasoning.
- Correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect licences, privacy, and safety.
- Avoid discriminatory, sexual, threatening, or harassing content.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment or intimidation;
- discrimination;
- sexualized conduct;
- doxxing or sharing private information;
- deliberate scientific misinformation;
- plagiarism or licence evasion;
- unsafe instructions presented irresponsibly;
- abuse of project/provider infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation. Before public community growth, the project must add explicit maintainer contact and enforcement procedure.
