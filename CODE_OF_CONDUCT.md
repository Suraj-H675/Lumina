# Code of Conduct

Lumina contributors are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive, evidence-based environment.

- Critique ideas and implementation choices, not people.
- Be patient with different experience levels.
- Prefer reproducible evidence over authority or confidence.
- Do not publish another person's private information.
- Do not use project spaces for harassment, threats, discrimination, or spam.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations.
