# UI Copy and Scientific Language Style

## Voice

Lumina is curious, calm, direct, and honest.

Use:

- “Here is what we know.”
- “This value is estimated.”
- “From your location, Jupiter reaches about 48°.”
- “Weather may still prevent observation.”
- “Scientists are not yet certain.”

Avoid:

- exaggerated hype;
- “mind-blowing” on every page;
- fake certainty;
- childish baby talk;
- generic AI language;
- unnecessary jargon;
- fear-based asteroid/space-weather language.

## Titles

Prefer question/action titles:

- What can you see tonight?
- Explore Saturn's rings
- Why stars have different colors
- Plan a 60-minute session

Avoid vague titles such as “Insights” or “Intelligence.”

## Numbers

- include units;
- round to source/model precision;
- use localized number formatting;
- scientific notation in Deep Dive when appropriate;
- define magnitude scale;
- give a comparison for extreme scale without replacing the real value.

## Dates and time

- show exact date for events;
- show time zone;
- distinguish event date and publication date;
- label tentative launch windows;
- do not say “today” in archived/static content without generated current context.

## Freshness

Use:

- Updated 12 minutes ago
- Data observed at 14:00 UTC
- Cached data from July 24, 2026
- Provider temporarily unavailable

Do not call data live merely because it came from an API.

## Errors

Good:

> We could not update launch data. The last successful update was July 25 at 08:00 UTC.

Bad:

> Oops! Something went wrong.

## Visibility

Good:

> Astronomically well placed between 9:10 PM and 11:30 PM. Cloud cover is not included.

Bad:

> Guaranteed visible tonight.

## Simulations

Use:

> This simplified model assumes two isolated bodies and ignores atmospheric drag.

Do not use:

> This is exactly how the real mission will behave.

## Images

Visible labels:

- Photograph
- Composite image
- False color
- Simulation
- Artist's concept
- Diagram

## Calls to action

Use concrete verbs:

- Explore
- Compare
- See tonight
- Add to plan
- Start experiment
- View sources
- Save locally
- Export journal

Avoid “Learn more” when a more specific action exists.

## Child/teen safety

Do not ask users to share public location or identity. External project links say:

> This opens an external citizen-science website.

Solar content always includes an appropriate safety callout.
